Design a complete high-end AI-powered career and personalized learning platform called:

SKILLFORGE AI

Tagline:
"Turn your skills into your career."

PRODUCT PURPOSE:
SkillForge AI helps college students become placement-ready by analyzing their resume, skills, projects, learning progress, target job roles, and interview performance.

The platform should:
1. Upload and analyze resumes
2. Extract and verify skills
3. Allow students to select a target career/job
4. Analyze job descriptions
5. Calculate personalized skill gaps
6. Generate adaptive learning roadmaps
7. Recommend courses
8. Recommend projects
9. Recommend certifications
10. Generate interview questions
11. Conduct AI mock interviews
12. Evaluate interview answers
13. Track learning progress
14. Calculate placement readiness
15. Analyze GitHub portfolio
16. Dynamically update the roadmap based on assessments and progress
17. Provide an admin dashboard for platform management and analytics

IMPORTANT PRODUCT DIFFERENTIATOR:

SkillForge AI should NOT look like a generic chatbot or course platform.

The central experience is:

RESUME

SKILL EXTRACTION
